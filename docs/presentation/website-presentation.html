Coolvane HVACWebsite & platform overview
01 · Executive summary

A site that argues the way an engineer argues: with measured numbers.

Coolvane designs, installs and maintains split, VRF, chilled-water and precision cooling systems across Dallas, Fort Worth and Plano. The website mirrors that method — every page states capacity, lead time, scope, exclusions and the measurement taken at handover. Nothing is claimed that a commissioning sheet could not prove.

02 · Design system

One component library. Two complete atmospheres.

The interface is built on semantic tokens, not fixed colours. Every component reads roles — surface, panel, ink block, foreground, hairline, accent — so the entire site can change character without editing a single component.

THEME A

Foundry — engineered, dark

The default. Blueprint grid, Clash Display headlines, JetBrains Mono figures, cyan and ember accents. Chosen for plant rooms, commissioning sheets and technical buyers.

THEME B

Atelier — editorial, light

Bone paper, Instrument Serif headlines, teal and copper accents, no grid. For daylight reading, print-like proposals and owner-side audiences.

Contrast is enforced per theme. Faint labels, hairlines and ink blocks each resolve to the correct value in both atmospheres, so a section that reads well in dark mode cannot dissolve into white in light mode.
Motion language
SCROLL

DEPTH

RESTRAINT

Every effect has a reduced-motion fallback, and no content depends on animation to be read.

Coolvane HVACCustomer journey
04 · Customer journey

From a setpoint to a signed scope, in six steps.

Every visual decision serves one of these steps. The site is not a brochure that ends at a phone number; it is the first stage of the delivery process, and the data it captures enters the admin portal immediately.

StepVisitor intentWhat the page doesSystem action
01“Can they hold a setpoint?”Hero states the design intent and the measured handover standard—
02“Do they do my system?”Service stack and full index filter by category and capacity—
03“What will it cost?”Sizing helper produces an indicative load in TR and attaches it to the briefCalculation carried into the lead
04“Is the scope honest?”Includes and excludes shown at equal weight, pricing basis stated—
05“Who answers?”Direct line, emergency dispatch, response targets by plan—
06“Let’s talk.”Callback panel and full enquiry form with validationLead created, tagged with service and source
CONVERSION

Two entry points, one inbox

A thirty-second callback request in the hero and a detailed brief on the service page both land in the same lead record, with source and service context preserved.

MEASURED

No invented proof

Testimonials are labelled sample copy until moderated records replace them. No aggregate rating is displayed before real reviews exist.

Coolvane HVACAdmin portal
07 · Admin portal — pipeline

Every enquiry arrives already qualified.

The portal is the operational half of the website. A lead is not an email in an inbox: it carries its source, service, city, urgency, plan and any sizing calculation attached on the public page.

DASHBOARD

Today, in one screen

Open leads by stage, overdue invoices, reminders due, monthly collected versus invoiced, and payroll commitment — the four numbers that decide the week.

LEADS

A pipeline, not a list

New, contacted, site visit, quoted, won, lost. Owner, value, follow-up date and a timestamped note trail per lead, with manual entry for phone enquiries.

CUSTOMERS

One record per site

Contact details, city, job history and invoice links, so a repeat call is answered with the plant history already open.

REMINDERS

Nothing quietly expires

Follow-ups, renewal windows and payment chases, each linked back to a lead or invoice and cleared when done.

08 · Admin portal — revenue & operations

Quotation to settlement, without a second system.

Invoicing, payments, expenses, team and payroll live beside the pipeline. The same brand billing identity printed on the public site is used on every invoice, and nothing needs exporting to a spreadsheet to be understood.

ModuleWhat it holdsWhat it answers
InvoicesLine items, discount, tax mode, due date, terms, notes, payment allocationWho owes what, and since when
PaymentsAmount, method, reference, date received, recorded byIs the balance real, partial or settled
ExpensesDate, vendor, category, amountWhere the month went, by category
TeamRole, status, monthly salaryWho is active and what the commitment is
PayrollPeriod, base, bonus, deductions, methodWhat is pending this month
RemindersTitle, due time, linked lead or invoiceWhat must happen next
PRINT

Invoice that reads like a document

A print-ready sheet with the legal entity, billing identity, itemised scope, tax, balance and terms — generated from live data.

SAFETY

Guards on money

Overpayment is rejected against the open balance. Cancelled invoices accept no further payments. Every mutation is admin-only.

SUMMARY

Roll-ups, not guesswork

Invoiced, collected, outstanding, overdue and this-month figures are computed server-side from the same records.

No payment gateway and no processing fee: settlement is recorded as cash, cheque, card on site or bank transfer, matched against the invoice reference.

09 · Managing the site without a developer

Everything visible to a visitor is editable from the portal.

The public site reads a single content document. The studio edits that document, saves a revision and publishes. No build step, no deployment, no developer in the loop for copy, pricing, imagery or a new service.

FLOW

Edit, review, publish

Draft state is validated against the same schema the API uses, saved as a revision, then published. Unpublished changes never reach the public site.

REACH

One source, every surface

Header, footer, coverage, invoices, sitemap and structured data all read the same document — so a phone number is corrected once, not in six places.

10 · Benefits, security & next steps

What the business gets, and what keeps it safe.

The value is not the animation. It is that the website and the operation share one set of facts — and that the people who own those facts can change them without a project.

COMMERCIAL

Fewer unqualified enquiries

Capacity, lead time, pricing basis and exclusions are stated before the call, so conversations start at scope rather than at education.

OPERATIONAL

One workspace

Lead, quote, invoice, payment and payroll follow the same customer record. No re-keying between systems.

MARKETING

Publish in minutes

A new service, price change or journal article is live without a developer, a deployment window or a change request.

TRUST

Evidence over adjectives

Measured values, named standards and written exclusions do more for credibility than any claim on a banner.
